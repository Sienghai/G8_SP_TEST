Topic 1 · B1 Level
Lifestyle & Work
Date
Part 1 Introduction & Interview — 4–5 minutes
The teacher will ask you general questions. Try to answer in 2–3 sentences.
  • What would you do if you didn't have to work? How would you spend your time?
  • If you could buy a house anywhere in the world, where would you choose? Why?
  • If you could change one thing about your lifestyle, what would you change?
  • If you could choose any job, which would you choose and why?
  • Do you think a good work–life balance is important? How do you try to achieve it?
Part 2 Individual Long Turn — 3–4 minutes
You have 1 minute to prepare, then speak for 1–2 minutes.
Task Card
Talk about your ideal lifestyle.
  • – Where you would live and what kind of home you would have
  • – What your typical day would look like
  • – How much time you would spend working and relaxing
  • – Why this lifestyle would make you happy
You have 1 minute to get ready. Then speak for 1–2 minutes.
Part 3 Two-Way Discussion — 4–5 minutes
The teacher will ask bigger questions. Give longer answers with reasons and examples.
  • Do you agree that we should have more small shops and fewer shopping centres and supermarkets? Why?
  • How has the idea of work–life balance changed over the last 20 years?
  • Do you think people today place too much importance on money and career? Why?
  • What do you think governments could do to help people have a better work–life balance?

Topic 2 · B1 Level
Technology & the Environment
Date
Part 1 Introduction & Interview — 4–5 minutes
The teacher will ask you general questions. Try to answer in 2–3 sentences.
  • What are some advantages of not having a mobile phone? Could you live without one?
  • What do you personally do to help protect the environment?
  • What do you think is the most useful invention of the last 50 years? Why?
  • Do you believe some people are luckier than others, or do we make our own luck?
  • How do you feel when you receive too much information at once? How do you deal with it?
Part 2 Individual Long Turn — 3–4 minutes
You have 1 minute to prepare, then speak for 1–2 minutes.
Task Card
Talk about a piece of technology that has changed your life.
  • – What the technology is and when you started using it
  • – How it has changed the way you work or live
  • – Whether the changes have been mostly positive or negative
  • – How life would be different without it
You have 1 minute to get ready. Then speak for 1–2 minutes.
Part 3 Two-Way Discussion — 4–5 minutes
The teacher will ask bigger questions. Give longer answers with reasons and examples.
  • Do you agree that you should always help a stranger? Why or why not?
  • Is information overload a serious problem in modern life? What can people do about it?
  • How can technology help or harm the environment? Can you give examples of both?
  • Do you think people will become more or less dependent on technology in the future? Why?
Topic 3 · B1 Level
Architecture & Design
Date
Part 1 Introduction & Interview — 4–5 minutes
The teacher will ask you general questions. Try to answer in 2–3 sentences.
  • What is a famous building that you really like? What makes it special?
  • Are there any types of modern design that you dislike? Why?
  • Can you describe an advertisement that you think is very clever?
  • Has your wallet or bag ever been stolen? What happened?
  • Do you like watching detective series or films? Why or why not?
Part 2 Individual Long Turn — 3–4 minutes
You have 1 minute to prepare, then speak for 1–2 minutes.
Task Card
Talk about a building or place that you find beautiful or interesting.
  • – What the building or place is and where it is
  • – How you first came to know about it
  • – What makes it special or unique in your opinion
  • – Whether you think modern buildings are as beautiful as older ones
You have 1 minute to get ready. Then speak for 1–2 minutes.
Part 3 Two-Way Discussion — 4–5 minutes
The teacher will ask bigger questions. Give longer answers with reasons and examples.
  • Do you agree that architecture was much more beautiful a hundred years ago? Why?
  • Do you think designers should always look to the future rather than the past? Why?
  • How important is it for a city to preserve its old buildings and architecture?
  • How do you think the buildings we live and work in affect our wellbeing and happiness?
